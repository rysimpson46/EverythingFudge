Made by one person
who knows
exactly what she's doing.

This business started in a home kitchen, with a recipe that had been in the family for a long time. No commercial equipment, no investor money, no consultants. Just a stove, a thermometer, and a belief that people can taste the difference between something made with care and something made for margin.

Decades later, very little has changed — which is exactly the point. The recipe is the same. The ingredients are real. The batches are still small enough that every single one gets our full attention.
