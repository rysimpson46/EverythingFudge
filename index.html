Our Story

Made by hand,
with intention.

Everything Fudge started in a home kitchen in 1987 with one pan, one recipe, and no shortcuts. The original batch was shared with neighbors. Word spread. The recipe stayed the same.

We still cook everything by hand, still cut every slab by hand, and still refuse to automate anything that matters. The result is fudge that tastes like someone made it — because someone did.

We make four flavors. We have made them for over thirty years. We do not plan to add another one unless it earns its place.

"The test was always the same: does it make you close your eyes when you eat it? If not, we are not done yet."
— The Founder
